NOVA TECH Social Media Policy — South edition

POSTING RULES
Posts on X must stay under 280 characters with at most two hashtags.
Do not use the words 'cheap' or 'budget laptop' in any post.

TONE
Never use more than one exclamation mark. Avoid slang such as OMG or insane.

DISCLOSURE
Paid creator posts must be labelled #ad.
